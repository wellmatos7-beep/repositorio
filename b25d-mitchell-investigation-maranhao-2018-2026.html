The North American B-25D Mitchell investigation on the Maranhão coast (2018–2026)

English reference version of the Portuguese dossier. Full evidence, records and verification are documented in the book A II Guerra Mundial no Chão do Brasil (2026), ISBN 978-65-02-07804-4, by Wellington Matos (ISNI 0000 0005 3060 6430).

Researcher: Wellington Matos (Well Matos) · Site: Humberto de Campos – Baía do Tubarão – Canal da Tábua – Maranhão – Brazil · Period: 2018–2026

1. Overview

This dossier documents the historical-geographical investigation of the wreckage of a U.S. North American B-25D Mitchell bomber that crashed on the Maranhão coast during World War II.

Conducted by Wellington Matos, the research combined family memory, community testimonies, U.S. WWII repositories, Brazilian public archives, territorial survey, detection equipment and coordination with local and public agents.

It gained national attention in July 2021, when the remains near Humberto de Campos were publicized. All records and proof are compiled in the 2026 book — the main bibliographic reference for this chronology.

2. Historical context

During WWII the Brazilian coast was strategic for Allied air and naval operations in the South Atlantic. The Northeast — routes linking the United States, Belém and Natal — formed the Brazil–Africa air bridge toward North Africa, Europe and the Mediterranean.

In Maranhão this left material and immaterial traces: aircraft accidents, testimonies, wreckage and intergenerational memories — including the B-25 Mitchell near the Canal da Tábua, Humberto de Campos.

3. Chronology

“When I was a boy, during the war, a pair of airplanes always crossed the sky of São Bento. Sometimes toward the sunset, sometimes toward the sunrise.” — Seu Jarson (father of Wellington).
  1. 2018 · First milestone

    SAGRIMA / SIGITE / ZEE. As a superintendent at SAGRIMA, Matos created the SIGITE geoprocessing laboratory and joined the Legal Amazon and Maranhão Ecological-Economic Zoning (ZEE) committees — the technical basis for later mapping.

  2. 2018 · Founding testimony

    Humberto de Campos: Mr. Josélio. During fieldwork, Matos met Mr. Josélio (foster son of Vanda Araújo), who first reported an aircraft crash in the municipality during WWII — the starting point of the investigation.

  3. 2020 · Documentary breakthrough

    After old newspapers yielded nothing, Matos searched U.S. WWII repositories, identified the B-25D accident report and began complementary research in public archives. References are in the 2026 book.

  4. 2021 · Field survey

    While conducting a private georeferencing service at Fazenda Mato Grosso, he visited the crash area with local technician and witness Júnior Vasconcelos.

  5. 2021 · Institutional step

    At SETRES, he registered the find, organized a planning workshop on productive arrangements for Humberto de Campos, presented the discovery in plenary and formed a recovery commission with municipal staff.

  6. 2021 · Wreckage located

    Using equipment with fishermen, riverine communities and residents, the wreckage was located [Baía do Tubarão / Canal da Tábua area]. National press coverage followed; archival research deepened and the book project was born.

  7. 2026 · Book launch

    A II Guerra Mundial no Chão do Brasil launched at the 28th São Paulo Book Biennial, Belo Horizonte Flip (MG) and 19th FeliS – São Luís (MA).

  8. 2026 · Further milestones

    Feature-film script Sementes da Guerra; planned recovery of the PBY Catalina wreckage; admission to the IHGM (Historical and Geographical Institute of Maranhão); proposal of the WWII Memorial in Maranhão.
